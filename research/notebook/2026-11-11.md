## Observations (smoke runs; not E001 results)
- E001 runner smoke test (synthetic, CPU, uncommitted tree; `runs/E001-pooled-attention-fidelity/*-smoke*`):
  - With equal-size regions and **no text tokens**, the log m bias is a constant logit shift and
    cancels in softmax: `rel_err_logm == rel_err_nobias` (0.17 at g=2).
  - With 256 text tokens and ragged edges (23×30 grid), the median relative output error is
    **0.14 with log m vs 0.73 without** at g=2, and 0.16 vs 1.53 at g=8.
  - So the bias matters exactly when stand-ins compete with entries of a different size: text,
    resident exact tokens, edge regions. E001 phase B must include the text KV in each dump; it now
    reads `text_k/text_v`.
  - The covariance correction (r=4) cuts the region log-mass MAE from 0.014 to 0.0005 at g=2.
    Top-8 recall is already above 0.98 at r=0 on synthetic keys, so real keys are needed to
    see whether r matters for ranking.
